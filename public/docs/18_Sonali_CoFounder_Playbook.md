## Phase 0: Months 1-3 — Community Building (Zero Cost)

Zero Budget Community-First Brand Foundation

Time commitment: 10-15 hours per week alongside full-time employment.

Weeks 1-2: LinkedIn Strategy Launch

#### Establish PhDSetu's Voice on LinkedIn

  * **Day 1 -2:** Update LinkedIn headline to "Co-Founder & CEO, PhDSetu | Building India's PhD Career Bridge | IIT Bombay PhD | Industry Scientist"
  * **Day 3 -4:** Publish long-form article: "Why India's 35,000 PhD Graduates a Year Deserve Better Career Infrastructure"
  * **Day 5 -7:** Post 3 short-form posts — share PhD career statistics, tag IIT Bombay alumni, start conversations
  * **Week 2:** Launch a 5-part LinkedIn series "The PhD Career Crisis" — one post every 2 days with data, personal stories, and a call to join PhDSetu
  * **Daily (30 min):** Engage meaningfully on 10-15 posts from PhD scholars, professors, and HR leaders. Comment with value, not just likes
  * **Week 2 End:** Target 200+ new connections from PhD/research/academia community



Weeks 3-4: WhatsApp Community Launch

#### Build the Founding Community of 50 Scholars

  * **Day 15 -16:** Create PhDSetu WhatsApp Community with welcome message, rules, and value proposition. Set up structured groups: General, Career Resources, Job Alerts, Events
  * **Day 17 -19:** Personally invite 30 PhD scholars from IIT Bombay network — send individual DMs explaining the vision, not mass invites
  * **Day 20 -22:** Post a LinkedIn call-to-action: "We're building India's first career support community for PhD scholars. First 50 founding members get lifetime premium access." Share WhatsApp join link
  * **Week 4:** Host first informal virtual meetup (Zoom, 30 min) — "What Do PhD Scholars Really Need?" Collect pain points. Use insights for content
  * **Week 4 End:** Target 50+ scholars in WhatsApp community, 5+ active conversations per day



Month 2: Content Escalation & First Webinar

#### Establish Thought Leadership

  * **Week 5:** Launch first LinkedIn Live / Audio Event — "Ask Me Anything: Transitioning from PhD to Industry" (leverage personal experience at BARC, Nivea, Momentive)
  * **Week 6:** Start weekly content series "PhD Success Stories" — interview 1 PhD scholar who transitioned to industry per week, post their story on LinkedIn
  * **Week 7:** Co-host a LinkedIn Live with a friendly HR leader or recruiter from your network about "What Employers Actually Want from PhDs"
  * **Week 8:** First PhDSetu webinar — "Cracking the Industry Code: A Masterclass for PhD Scholars" — free, registration through Google Forms, promote on LinkedIn and WhatsApp
  * **Ongoing:** 4 LinkedIn posts per week (2 value/insights, 1 PhD success story, 1 PhDSetu update). 30-minute daily engagement routine
  * **Month 2 End:** Target 150+ WhatsApp members, 500+ LinkedIn followers growth, 80+ webinar registrations



Month 3: Scale to 300+ & First External Conversations

#### Critical Mass & Partnership Seeds

  * **Week 9 -10:** Launch referral drive — "Invite 3 PhD friends, get featured as PhDSetu Community Champion." Create shareable graphics for WhatsApp and LinkedIn
  * **Week 10:** First employer conversation — reach out to 3 HR leaders in your network (Momentive, Aditya Birla, etc.) for informal chats about PhD hiring challenges
  * **Week 11:** First university contact — email career services at IIT Bombay, IIT Madras, IISc. Introduce PhDSetu as a career support partner for PhD students
  * **Week 12:** Community milestone celebration — LinkedIn post celebrating 300 members, tagging active members, sharing early wins and testimonials
  * **Month 3 End:** 300+ WhatsApp members, 3+ employer warm leads, 2+ university contacts initiated, 2 webinars completed, 1,000+ LinkedIn connection growth



**Phase 0 Success Gate:** Before moving to Phase 1, validate: (1) 300+ engaged scholars in community, (2) At least 2 successful webinars, (3) NPS score of 7+ from community survey, (4) 3+ warm employer leads, (5) Co-founders aligned on MVP requirements. If any criterion is not met, extend Phase 0 by 4 weeks.

## Conflict Resolution Protocol

**When Co-Founders Disagree:**  
  
**Step 1 — Voice & Listen (Same Day):** Each person states their position and reasoning clearly. The others listen without interrupting. Use "I believe X because Y" format.  
  
**Step 2 — Data Check (24 Hours):** If the disagreement is fact-based, collect data. Let the data decide, not opinions.  
  
**Step 3 — Domain Deference:** If the decision falls primarily in one founder's domain (partnerships = Sonali, tech = Trupti, strategy = Radha), the domain expert gets final call after hearing others.  
  
**Step 4 — Majority Vote:** For cross-domain decisions where data is ambiguous, two out of three agree.  
  
**Step 5 — External Advisor:** For truly stuck decisions, bring in a trusted advisor or mentor for a tie-breaking perspective. Never let a disagreement fester beyond 1 week.  
  
**Sacred Rule:** Once a decision is made, all three founders support it publicly — even if they disagreed privately. Unified front, always.

**Brand Risk: Employer Association**  
Sonali's current employer may object to public startup activity | High | Medium |  Review employment contract for non-compete and moonlighting clauses. Keep PhDSetu activities strictly outside work hours. Do not use employer resources, name, or connections without permission. LinkedIn bio should not mention current employer in PhDSetu context. Consider informing HR proactively if contract allows it. Have legal counsel review before any public PR.   
**Time Management: Full-Time Job + Startup**  
Trying to do both could lead to underperformance at either | High | High |  Strict time-boxing: 15 hrs/week max in Phase 0. Use the weekly template religiously. Delegate community management to moderators early. Agree with co-founders on a clear "full-time trigger" — the revenue/funding milestone at which Sonali transitions. Track weekly hours in a simple spreadsheet. If consistently exceeding 20 hrs without plan to transition, pause and re-evaluate.   
**Burnout Risk**  
CEO + full-time + family = recipe for exhaustion | High | Medium |  Sunday is sacred — minimal work. One full weekend off per month (no PhDSetu at all). Monthly founder check-in explicitly discusses energy levels. "Sustainable pace" as a core value: if any founder shows burnout signs, the other two take on extra load for 2 weeks. Build systems and delegation early — no single point of failure on Sonali for community operations.   
**Community Toxicity**  
WhatsApp groups can devolve into complaint forums or spam | Medium | Medium |  Establish clear community guidelines from Day 1 (see Section 7). Appoint moderators early. Use WhatsApp Community features to control who can post in announcement channels. Address negativity quickly but empathetically — validate concerns, then redirect to action. Remove persistent bad actors without hesitation. Weekly "community pulse" check — are conversations constructive?   
**Partnership Rejection**  
Universities and employers may not see value early on | Medium | High |  Expect 80% rejection rate — this is normal for early-stage partnerships. Track all outreach in CRM with status and follow-up dates. Ask "no" responders: "What would need to be true for you to reconsider?" Use early partners as social proof. Start with warm network (IIT Bombay, Momentive connections) before cold outreach. Offer free pilots to reduce risk for partners.   
**Co-Founder Misalignment**  
Different visions, speeds, or commitment levels between founders | High | Low |  Monthly strategy meeting includes explicit alignment check. Founders agreement document covers: equity vesting, decision-making, exit clauses, commitment expectations. Quarterly "founder therapy" — 2-hour session where each person shares frustrations honestly without judgment. If misalignment persists beyond 2 months, bring in a mentor/advisor for mediation.   
**Market Timing Risk**  
PhD career support may not be a large enough market for VCs | Medium | Medium |  Frame PhDSetu as "vertical talent platform" not just "PhD career support." Position alongside LinkedIn (horizontal) vs. PhDSetu (vertical, expert talent). Highlight adjacent markets: postdocs, research associates, STEM masters. Build data moat early — proprietary PhD career data is valuable to employers and policymakers. Pursue grant/CSR funding in parallel to VC to reduce dependence.
