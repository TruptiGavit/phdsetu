Section 05 Signature Project

## PhD Career Report Playbook

PhDSetu's annual PhD Career Report is Radha's signature intellectual contribution — the platform's "State of the Union" for doctoral careers in India. This is the single most important strategic asset PhDSetu will produce.

◆ Why This Report Matters

No comparable report exists in India. The PhD Career Report becomes PhDSetu's definitive voice — cited by media, referenced by government, and used by every university career office. It is simultaneously a research contribution, a marketing asset, a policy tool, and a community service. If PhDSetu does nothing else in its first year, this report justifies the organisation's existence.

### Research Methodology Design

  * **Study type:** Cross-sectional descriptive survey with purposive and snowball sampling
  * **Population:** PhD scholars and recent graduates (within 5 years) across all disciplines in India
  * **Mixed methods:** Quantitative survey (primary) + qualitative interviews (supplementary, N=50-100)
  * **Ethical clearance:** Apply through Radha's university IRB + PhDSetu Ethics Board review
  * **Data privacy:** GDPR-aligned consent, anonymization protocols, secure storage
  * **Limitations disclosure:** Transparent about sampling biases, response rates, generalizability
  * **Longitudinal design:** Year 1 establishes baseline; subsequent years track trends



### Survey Questionnaire Structure

  * **Section A:** Demographics — age, gender, discipline, institution type, year of PhD, geography
  * **Section B:** Career trajectory — current employment, role type, time-to-employment, sector
  * **Section C:** Skills & preparedness — skills gap analysis, industry readiness self-assessment
  * **Section D:** Support systems — career guidance received, mentorship access, institutional support
  * **Section E:** Career satisfaction — alignment of PhD training with career outcomes
  * **Section F:** Barriers & challenges — open-ended questions on systemic obstacles
  * **Section G:** Aspirations — what would have helped, what they wish existed
  * Estimated completion time: 15-20 minutes



### Sampling Strategy — Reaching 5,000+ Scholars

  * **University partners:** Distribute through 15-20 partner institutions' internal channels
  * **PhDSetu community:** Existing registered scholars (warm audience, high response rate)
  * **LinkedIn campaign:** Targeted ads + organic posts to PhD/researcher audiences
  * **Professional societies:** Indian Science Congress, discipline-specific associations
  * **Alumni networks:** IIT, IISc, central university alumni groups
  * **Snowball:** Each respondent shares with 3-5 peers (incentivize with early access to results)
  * **Regional language support:** Hindi and 2-3 regional language versions for state university reach
  * **Target: 5,000 responses minimum** with stratification by discipline, institution type, and region



### Data Analysis Framework

  * **Descriptive statistics:** Frequencies, cross-tabs, demographic breakdowns
  * **Comparative analysis:** By discipline, institution tier, gender, geography
  * **Skills gap index:** Composite score measuring PhD-industry skills alignment
  * **Time-to-employment analysis:** Survival curves by discipline and institution type
  * **Satisfaction index:** Multi-dimensional career satisfaction composite
  * **Qualitative coding:** Thematic analysis of open-ended responses using NVivo
  * **Visualization:** Professional charts, maps, infographics designed for media use
  * **Confidence intervals and significance testing** for all key findings



Report Component| Description| Audience  
**Executive Summary** | 4-page visual brief with top 10 findings, key statistics, and headline recommendations | Media, policymakers, busy academics  
**Full Report** | 60-80 page comprehensive analysis with methodology, detailed findings, cross-tabulations, and policy recommendations | Researchers, university administrators, government  
**Infographic Deck** | 20-slide visual deck optimized for social media sharing and presentations | Social media, conferences, community  
**Policy Brief** | 8-page document framing findings as policy recommendations for NEP 2020 and ANRF alignment | DST, UGC, ministry officials, ANRF

**Data Tables** | Open-access anonymized data tables for researchers to conduct further analysis | Academic researchers, think tanks

## Impact Measurement Framework

A rigorous, evidence-based impact framework that demonstrates PhDSetu's value to scholars, institutions, investors, and government — grounded in Radha's expertise in programme evaluation and social impact assessment.

◆ Theory of Change for PhDSetu

**Problem:** 2,12,000+ PhD scholars in India lack dedicated career infrastructure — no centralized platform for career guidance, industry connection, skill translation, or peer networking.  
**Inputs:** Technology platform, career content, community building, industry partnerships, university relationships, research evidence.  
**Activities:** Profile building, skill mapping, job matching, mentorship connection, career workshops, PhD Career Report publication, policy advocacy.  
**Outputs:** Registered scholars, completed profiles, job applications, mentorship sessions, workshop attendance, report downloads.  
**Outcomes:** Reduced time-to-employment, improved career satisfaction, expanded career awareness, strengthened industry-academia connection, informed policy decisions.  
**Impact:** A transformed PhD career landscape in India where every doctoral scholar has access to the infrastructure needed to translate their research into meaningful careers.

Section 08 Governance

## Institutional Design & Governance

Radha architects PhDSetu's governance framework — ensuring the organisation is built for long-term institutional credibility, not just short-term startup agility.

### Section 8 Company Governance Framework

If PhDSetu registers as a Section 8 (not-for-profit) company, Radha's governance framework includes:

  * **Board of Directors:** 3 founders + 2-3 independent directors (academic and industry)
  * **Board meetings:** Minimum quarterly (Section 8 mandate), with strategic review agenda
  * **Annual General Meeting:** Compliance with Companies Act, 2013 requirements
  * **Financial oversight:** Annual audit, CSR compliance, FCRA registration planning
  * **Founder agreements:** Equity/role clarity, vesting schedules, exit provisions
  * **Amendments:** MOA/AOA drafting with objects clause aligned to PhD career support



### Board Composition Recommendations

  * **Founding Board (Year 1):** Sonali (CEO), Radha (CSO), Trupti (CTO) + 1 independent director
  * **Expanded Board (Year 2):** Add 2 independent directors — 1 from academia (Vice-Chancellor level), 1 from industry (CHRO or CSR head)
  * **Diversity mandate:** Minimum 33% women directors (already met with 3 women founders)
  * **Advisory overlap:** Advisory board members are not board directors (keep governance clean)
  * **Board skills matrix:** Ensure coverage of legal, financial, academic, technology, and policy expertise
  * **Compensation:** Board members serve pro bono initially; revisit at revenue milestone



### Ethics Board Structure

Critical for research credibility and data trust:

  * **Composition:** 3-5 members — research ethics expert, data privacy specialist, student advocate, industry representative
  * **Chair:** External academic with IRB experience (not a founder)
  * **Mandate:** Review all surveys, data collection, research publications, and partnerships involving scholar data
  * **Meeting frequency:** Quarterly, plus ad-hoc review of specific research instruments
  * **Key policies:** Informed consent templates, data retention policy, anonymization standards, conflict-of-interest declaration
  * **Independence:** Ethics board can veto any data use or research activity



### Academic Advisory Board Design

  * **Size:** 8-12 members, diverse by discipline, geography, and institution type
  * **Profile mix:** 3-4 senior academics (Professors/Deans), 2-3 industry PhD leaders, 2 policy experts, 1-2 international advisors
  * **Engagement model:** 2 advisory meetings/year (virtual) + individual expert consultations
  * **Value exchange:** Advisors get visibility, access to data, and PhDSetu credential; PhDSetu gets expertise, networks, and credibility
  * **Term:** 2-year renewable terms, staggered for continuity
  * **Recruitment priority:** Start with Radha and Sonali's existing academic networks
