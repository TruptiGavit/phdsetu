## Entity Structure Decision

India has no single legal form for social enterprises. PhDSetu's mission -- career infrastructure as a public good with sustainable commercial revenue -- demands a hybrid model: one entity holds the mission, the other drives the business.

### Comparison of Legal Structures

---|---|---|---|---|---  
**Governing Law** | Companies Act, 2013 | Indian Trusts Act, 1882 / State laws | Societies Reg. Act, 1860 | Companies Act, 2013 | Both statutes  
**Profit Distribution** | Prohibited | Prohibited | Prohibited | Allowed | Split by Entity  
**Equity Investment** | Not possible | Not possible | Not possible | Full flexibility | Via Pvt Ltd arm

### Decision: Hybrid Model -- Section 8 + Private Limited

A single entity cannot simultaneously accept CSR/grant funding (which requires non-profit status and prohibition on profit distribution) while also raising equity from investors and distributing returns. The hybrid model solves this by splitting PhDSetu into two co-operating entities under a unified brand and mission, connected through a formal Service Agreement.

### Key Terms of the Inter-Entity Service Agreement

  * **Brand License:** PhDSetu Foundation licenses the PhDSetu brand, trademarks, and Research Passport schema to PhDSetu Technologies Pvt. Ltd. for commercial use. License fee set at arm's length (typically 3-5% of gross revenue) to satisfy transfer pricing norms.
  * **Technology Services:** Pvt Ltd provides platform development, hosting, AI tools, and technical infrastructure to the Foundation at cost-plus margin (typically 10-15%).
  * **Revenue Share:** 10% of all recruiter fees collected by Pvt Ltd are transferred quarterly to the Foundation, earmarked for Deepstambh scholarship fund.
  * **Data Handling:** Foundation remains the Data Fiduciary for community/mentorship data. Pvt Ltd is the Data Fiduciary for commercial transaction data. Shared data governed by a Data Processing Agreement.

  * **Audit Rights:** Each entity has the right to audit the other's compliance with the agreement annually.
  * **Term & Renewal:** Initial 5-year term with automatic 3-year renewals. Either party may terminate with 12 months' written notice.
  * **Dispute Resolution:** Mediation first, then arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Seat of arbitration: Mumbai.

## Section 8 Company Formation

PhDSetu Foundation -- the non-profit arm -- is incorporated as a Section 8 Company under the Companies Act, 2013. As of 2026, Section 8 incorporation is fully integrated into the SPICe+ process on MCA V3, with no separate INC-12 application required.

### Step-by-Step Incorporation Timeline

Days 1-2

#### Obtain Digital Signature Certificates (DSC)

  * All proposed directors must obtain a Class 3 DSC from a licensed Certifying Authority (eMudhra, Sify, Capricorn)
  * Cost: ₹800-₹1,800 per director (1-2 year validity)
  * Required documents: PAN card, Aadhaar, passport-size photograph, email & mobile verification



Days 3-5

#### Name Reservation via SPICe+ Part A

  * Log into MCA V3 portal (mca.gov.in) and open SPICe+ Part A
  * Submit up to 2 proposed names -- e.g., "PhDSetu Foundation" and "PhDSetu Career Development Foundation"
  * Section 8 names typically end with Foundation, Forum, Association, Council, or similar
  * Fee: ₹1,000 for name reservation
  * Approved names are valid for 20 days -- Part B must be filed within this window
  * CRC (Central Registration Centre, Manesar) processes on faceless, randomised basis: 2-5 working days



Days 5-10

#### Draft MOA and AOA

  * **Memorandum of Association (eMOA / INC-33):** Must define objects clearly as charitable. Recommended objects clause: "To promote, develop, and facilitate career development, mentorship, skill building, employment readiness, and professional networking for doctoral research scholars across all disciplines in India, and to advance education, research, and social welfare related to the academic and professional ecosystems of PhD holders."
  * **Articles of Association (eAOA / INC-34):** Standard format with non-profit governance provisions -- no profit distribution, surplus applied to objects, winding-up assets transferred to similar Section 8 entity
  * Professional drafting fee: ₹10,000-₹20,000 (CS or CA assistance recommended)



Days 8-12

#### File SPICe+ Part B (Integrated Incorporation)

  * SPICe+ Part B consolidates: Incorporation application, DIN allotment (up to 3 directors), PAN, TAN, EPFO/ESIC registration, and bank account opening
  * Attach signed eMOA, eAOA, proof of registered office (utility bill < 2 months + ownership docs or NOC), director KYC documents
  * Section 8 license is now integrated -- no separate INC-12 filing required (post-2019 amendment)
  * Government fee: ₹0 for authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh (sufficient for Section 8)
  * Stamp duty: Exempted in most states for Section 8 companies



Days 12-25

#### ROC Processing & Certificate of Incorporation

  * CRC (Manesar, Haryana) reviews the application and may publish a public notice for objections
  * ROC processing takes 10-15 working days for Section 8
  * Upon approval: Certificate of Incorporation issued with unique CIN and Section 8 License Number
  * PAN and TAN issued simultaneously
  * Bank account opened automatically via AGILE-PRO-S with partner bank



Days 25-30

#### Post-Incorporation Compliance

  * Appoint statutory auditor within 30 days of incorporation (Board resolution required)
  * Apply for 12A and 80G registration via Form 10A on Income Tax e-Filing portal
  * Obtain DARPAN registration (ngo.darpan.gov.in) for government scheme eligibility
  * Set up statutory registers: Register of Members, Register of Directors, Minutes Books

## Private Limited Company Formation

PhDSetu Technologies Pvt. Ltd. -- the commercial arm -- can be incorporated in parallel with the Section 8 entity. The SPICe+ process for a standard Pvt Ltd is faster (7-10 working days) and well-established.

🏢

### Incorporation Essentials

  * **Form:** SPICe+ (same MCA V3 portal)
  * **Minimum Directors:** 2 (at least 1 Indian resident)
  * **Minimum Shareholders:** 2 (can be same as directors)
  * **Timeline:** 7-10 working days
  * **Cost:** ₹7,000-25,000 total
  * **Registered Office:** Must have a valid address in India with utility bill proof



📈

### Capital Structure Planning

  * **Authorised Capital:** ₹10-25 lakh (set high enough for future equity rounds -- increasing later costs ₹5,000+ in stamp duty per ₹5 lakh increment)
  * **Paid-up Capital:** Start with ₹1-5 lakh (minimum practical amount)
  * **Share Classes:** Consider equity shares + compulsorily convertible preference shares (CCPS) to facilitate future investor rounds
  * **Face Value:** ₹10/share is standard. 10,000 shares at ₹10 = ₹1 lakh paid-up capital



### Founder Equity Allocation

Shareholder| Allocation| Rationale  
---|---|---  
**Dr. Sonali (Founder)**|  60-70%| Sole visionary, full-time commitment, brand anchor  
**Co-Founder / CTO (if applicable)**|  10-15%| Technical co-founder building the platform  
**ESOP Pool**|  10-15%| Reserved for future employees, advisors, early team. Vest over 4 years with 1-year cliff.  
**Advisor Pool**|  3-5%| IIT Bombay brain trust, industry mentors. Vest over 2 years.  
**Future Investment (Unallocated)**|  5-10%| Reserved for angel/seed round dilution  
  
### ESOP Pool Best Practices

  * **Create the ESOP pool before the first external funding round** -- investors will expect 10-15% set aside and will not want their equity diluted for the pool post-investment
  * **Standard vesting schedule:** 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff (25% vests at 12 months, remaining vests monthly or quarterly)
  * **Exercise price:** Set at fair market value (FMV) at the time of grant, determined by a registered valuer under Section 56(2)(viib) of the Income Tax Act
  * **ESOP scheme must be approved by Board resolution and special resolution of shareholders**
  * **File with ROC:** PAS-3 (return of allotment) within 15 days of each ESOP exercise



### Registered Office Requirements

Practical Guidance

#### Both entities can share a registered office address initially

The Companies Act permits two companies to share the same registered office address. This reduces costs in the early stage. However, each entity must maintain separate statutory registers, records, and letterheads at the premises. A shared office also simplifies logistics for board meetings and audits. Consider a co-working space with a GST-compliant address or a home office with proper NOC documentation.

## Deepstambh Integration

Deepstambh is an existing NGO that provides the social mission layer for PhDSetu. Integration must be structured to protect both entities legally while enabling the "10% of recruiter fees fund first-generation women research scholars" commitment.

### MoU Structure: PhDSetu Foundation ↔ Deepstambh

### Memorandum of Understanding -- Key Clauses

  * **Purpose:** Collaborative agreement to fund scholarships for first-generation women research scholars using 10% of recruiter fee revenue collected by PhDSetu Technologies Pvt. Ltd., transferred via PhDSetu Foundation
  * **Fund Flow Mechanism:**
    * Pvt Ltd calculates 10% of gross recruiter fees quarterly
    * Pvt Ltd transfers this amount to PhDSetu Foundation's designated bank account
    * Foundation disburses to Deepstambh within 15 days of receipt, against approved scholarship proposals
    * All transfers documented with invoice + payment acknowledgement
  * **Reporting Obligations:**
    * Deepstambh submits quarterly utilisation reports within 30 days of quarter-end
    * Reports include: beneficiary details, scholarship amounts, academic outcomes, photographic evidence
    * Annual impact assessment submitted by September 30 each year
    * PhDSetu Foundation retains the right to audit Deepstambh's scholarship accounts annually
  * **Joint Branding Guidelines:**
    * Deepstambh may use "Supported by PhDSetu" on scholarship-related communications
    * PhDSetu may use "In partnership with Deepstambh" on impact reports and donor-facing materials
    * Neither entity may represent the other's activities as its own
    * Joint logo usage requires prior written approval
  * **Legal Separation:**
    * Deepstambh is an independent entity -- not a subsidiary, branch, or division of PhDSetu
    * No shared directors between PhDSetu Foundation board and Deepstambh governing body (to avoid conflict of interest under Companies Act)
    * Each entity maintains independent financials, auditors, and compliance
    * Liability of each entity is limited to its own activities -- explicit mutual indemnification clause
  * **CSR Compliance:** If any CSR funds from corporate donors are routed through PhDSetu Foundation to Deepstambh, Deepstambh must hold valid CSR-1 registration (Form CSR-1 on MCA portal). Without CSR-1, the expenditure is disqualified.
  * **Term:** 3-year initial term, renewable annually. Either party may exit with 6 months' written notice. Ongoing scholarship commitments honoured through completion upon exit.



### Critical: CSR-1 Registration for Deepstambh

Since April 2021, any entity receiving CSR funds as an implementing agency must hold a valid CSR-1 registration number obtained via Form CSR-1 on the MCA portal. Deepstambh must obtain this registration before any CSR-sourced funds are transferred. Requirements:

  * Must be registered as a Trust, Society, or Section 8 Company
  * Must have at least 3 years of experience in charitable activities
  * Must hold valid 12A and 80G registration
  * Non-compliance: Funds transferred to unregistered entities are treated as "unspent" and attract penalties

## DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (notified November 13, 2025) is India's first comprehensive data protection law. Full substantive enforcement begins May 13, 2027. PhDSetu must build compliance from day one.

### Enforcement Timeline

November 13, 2025 -- Phase I

#### Data Protection Board Established

DPBI constituted and operational. Rules 1, 2, and 17-21 effective immediately. Procedural framework in place.

November 13, 2026 -- Phase II

#### Consent Manager Registration

Rule 4 (Consent Managers) becomes enforceable. Entities operating as consent managers must register with DPBI. Relevant if PhDSetu uses third-party consent management tools.

May 13, 2027 -- Phase III (Full Enforcement)

#### All Substantive Obligations Enforceable

  * Notice & consent requirements (Rule 3)
  * Security safeguards (Rule 6)
  * Breach notification to DPBI + affected individuals (Rule 7) within 72 hours
  * Data Principal rights: access, correction, erasure, nomination
  * Significant Data Fiduciary duties: DPO appointment, DPIA
  * Penalties: up to ₹250 crore per violation

### Compliance Checklist

### Consent Architecture

  * Implement granular consent collection: separate toggles for profile sharing with employers, mentorship matching, marketing communications, Research Passport visibility
  * Consent must be free, specific, informed, and unconditional (no bundled consent)
  * Provide clear, plain-language privacy notice at every data collection point (in English + Hindi minimum)
  * Enable easy consent withdrawal -- one-click revocation, processed within 7 days
  * Maintain auditable consent logs with timestamps, version of privacy policy consented to, and method of collection
  * No pre-ticked checkboxes or default opt-ins



### Data Principal Rights

  * **Right to Access:** Scholars can download all their data in machine-readable format
  * **Right to Correction:** Self-service profile editing + request system for derived data
  * **Right to Erasure:** Account deletion pipeline -- complete removal within 30 days (except data retained under legal obligation)
  * **Right to Nominate:** Allow scholars to designate a nominee for their data in case of death or incapacity
  * **Grievance Redressal:** Appoint a designated Grievance Officer. Respond within 7 days. Display officer contact on the platform and in privacy policy.



### Breach Notification Procedure

  * **Step 1 -- Detection & Assessment:** Incident response team triages the breach within 6 hours. Classify severity: Low / Medium / High / Critical.
  * **Step 2 -- Containment:** Isolate affected systems. Revoke compromised credentials. Preserve forensic evidence.
  * **Step 3 -- DPBI Notification:** Notify the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. Use the prescribed electronic format.
  * **Step 4 -- Affected Individual Notification:** Notify all affected Data Principals (scholars, employers, mentors) without undue delay. Include: nature of breach, data affected, remedial steps taken, contact details of Grievance Officer.
  * **Step 5 -- Detailed Report:** Submit a comprehensive follow-up report to DPBI within 72 hours of the initial notification, containing root cause analysis, full scope of impact, and remediation plan.
  * **Penalty for non-notification:** Up to ₹150 crore.

### When to Appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Under the DPDP Act, DPO appointment is mandatory only for entities designated as **Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDF)** by the Central Government based on volume and sensitivity of data processed. PhDSetu is unlikely to be designated as an SDF in its early years. However, best practice is to:

  * Designate an internal "Privacy Lead" from day one (can be a founder or senior team member)
  * Formally appoint a DPO when monthly active users exceed 50,000 or when processing sensitive categories at scale
  * The DPO must be based in India, report directly to the Board, and represent the entity before DPBI
  * Even without SDF designation, having a DPO demonstrates compliance maturity to institutional partners and donors



### PhDSetu's Advantage: Adult Users Only

Since PhDSetu's primary users are PhD scholars (typically 23+ years old), the platform avoids the stringent requirements for processing children's data under Section 9 of the DPDP Act. No verifiable parental consent is required, and no restrictions on behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising for minors apply. This significantly simplifies the consent architecture and reduces compliance overhead.

## Terms of Service Framework

PhDSetu serves multiple user types with distinct legal relationships. The ToS must be structured as a master agreement with role-specific addenda.

🎓

### Scholar Account Terms

  * Account is personal and non-transferable
  * Scholar retains ownership of their Research Passport content (thesis abstracts, skills data, video pitch)
  * PhDSetu receives a non-exclusive, worldwide license to display, share (with recruiter/employer as per scholar's visibility settings), and use anonymised data for aggregate reports (PhD Career Report)
  * Scholar responsible for accuracy of information provided
  * Free tier access to community features. Premium features require subscription.
  * Account deletion available at any time -- data removed within 30 days per DPDP Act



💼

### Employer Account Terms

  * Employer agrees to non-discriminatory hiring practices
  * Job listings must be genuine, legal, and comply with applicable labour laws
  * Employer may access scholar profiles only through the platform -- no scraping, no off-platform contact without scholar consent
  * Subscription fees are non-refundable except as specified in the Employer Agreement
  * Employer data shared with scholars limited to: company name, role, location, salary range, requirements



🤝

### Mentor Account Terms

  * Mentors provide guidance as independent advisors -- PhDSetu is not liable for mentorship outcomes
  * Mentors are vetted but not employees of PhDSetu
  * Session recordings (if any) require mutual consent and are governed by separate consent mechanism
  * Mentor payment terms: TDS deducted at source under Section 194J (10% for professional services)
  * Conflict of interest disclosure: mentors must disclose if they are recruiting for their own organisation



🏛

### Institutional Subscription Terms

  * University/department subscribes on behalf of affiliated scholars
  * Institution appoints an admin who manages scholar onboarding
  * Scholar data created via institutional account remains the scholar's property -- portable after graduation
  * Institution receives anonymised aggregate reports on scholar engagement and outcomes
  * Annual subscription with auto-renewal (60-day cancellation notice)



### Cross-Cutting Legal Provisions

  * **Content Ownership:** Research Passport data is owned by the scholar. PhDSetu owns platform software, AI algorithms, design, and curated content. User-generated content (forum posts, reviews) is licensed to PhDSetu for platform use.
  * **Liability Limitations:** PhDSetu provides the platform "as is." No warranty on job placement outcomes, mentor advice quality, or career trajectory predictions. Total liability capped at fees paid in the preceding 12 months.
  * **Prohibited Uses:** No spam, harassment, fake profiles, data scraping, circumvention of platform access controls, or use for illegal purposes.
  * **Governing Law:** Laws of India. Courts of Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
  * **Dispute Resolution:** Attempt good-faith negotiation for 30 days. If unresolved, refer to binding arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Single arbitrator. Seat: Mumbai. Language: English. Arbitration fees shared equally.
  * **Force Majeure:** Neither party liable for delays due to events beyond reasonable control.
  * **Severability:** If any provision is held invalid, remaining provisions remain in full force.
  * **Modification:** PhDSetu may update ToS with 30-day prior notice. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

### Recruiter Subscription Contract -- Template Framework

  * **Scope of Service:** Access to PhDSetu's scholar database (filtered by discipline, skills, location, experience), job listing posting, Research Passport browsing, AI-matched candidate shortlists, direct messaging with scholars (subject to scholar consent)
  * **Subscription Tiers:** Define 2-3 tiers (e.g., Starter / Growth / Enterprise) with clear feature differentiation
  * **Placement Fee:** Success-based fee (typically 8-15% of first-year CTC) payable within 30 days of candidate joining. Placement confirmed after 30-day retention period.
  * **Payment Terms:** Subscription fees invoiced annually or quarterly. Net-30 payment terms. Late payment: 1.5% per month interest.
  * **Service Level Commitments:**
    * Platform uptime: 99.5% (excluding scheduled maintenance with 48-hour notice)
    * Response to support queries: within 24 hours on business days
    * Candidate match delivery: within 5 business days of search request
  * **Refund Policy:**
    * Subscription: Pro-rata refund for unused months if cancelled within first 90 days
    * Placement fee: Full refund if candidate leaves within 30 days of joining (retention guarantee)
    * No refunds after 90 days of subscription or after 30-day retention period for placements
  * **Data Sharing Boundaries:**
    * Employers see: Research Passport summary, skills map, career interests, location preference
    * Employers do NOT see: personal contact details (until scholar consents to connect), full thesis text, mentor interactions, salary history
    * Employers may not download, export, or share scholar data outside the platform
  * **Anti-Discrimination Clause:** Employer agrees to evaluate candidates without discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, disability, age, or marital status. Any reported discriminatory behaviour results in immediate account suspension and potential termination.
  * **Indemnification:** Employer indemnifies PhDSetu against claims arising from employer's hiring decisions, workplace practices, or job listing content.
  * **Confidentiality:** Both parties agree to keep each other's confidential business information private. Survives termination for 3 years.
  * **Termination:** Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. PhDSetu may terminate immediately for breach, including discriminatory practices or data misuse.

## Institutional Subscription Contracts

Framework for agreements with universities, IITs, departments, and research institutions subscribing to PhDSetu for their doctoral scholars.

### University/Department Subscription Agreement Framework

  * **Parties:** PhDSetu Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Platform") and the subscribing university/department ("Institution")
  * **Scope:** Platform access for Institution's enrolled doctoral scholars (verified through institutional email or admin-provided roster)
  * **Features Included:** Research Passport creation, AI Career Translator, mentorship matching, job board access, career workshops, department-level analytics dashboard
  * **Data Handling for Institutional Accounts:**
    * Institution provides only scholar name and institutional email for onboarding -- no bulk personal data transfer
    * Scholar builds their own profile -- scholar controls visibility settings
    * Institution receives only anonymised, aggregate data (e.g., "72% of your CS scholars created Research Passports; 15 received interview requests")
    * Individual scholar data never shared with Institution without explicit scholar consent
    * When scholar graduates: account converts to individual account, all data portable with scholar
  * **Outcome Reporting:**
    * Quarterly engagement report: signups, Research Passport completions, mentorship sessions, employer connections
    * Annual placement outcomes report (aggregate only): sectors, average CTC, time-to-employment
    * Benchmarking against platform-wide anonymised data (if 50+ scholars in cohort)
  * **Pricing:** Annual subscription, priced per scholar (tiered: <100 / 100-500 / 500+ scholars). Volume discounts. Introductory pricing for first year (up to 50% off for anchor institutions).
  * **Renewal:** Auto-renewal with 60-day cancellation notice before renewal date. Price escalation capped at 10% per year.
  * **NAAC/NIRF Value:** PhDSetu provides Institution with verifiable data that supports NAAC/NIRF placement and outcome metrics -- a key value proposition.

## Tax & Registration

Both entities require separate tax registrations and have distinct compliance obligations. Getting these right from incorporation prevents costly corrections later.

📜

### 12A Registration (Tax Exemption)

Exempts the Foundation's income from tax, provided it is applied solely for charitable purposes.

  * **Step 1:** File Form 10A on Income Tax e-Filing portal within 1 month of incorporation
  * **Step 2:** Provisional 12AB registration granted for 3 years
  * **Step 3:** Apply for regular registration via Form 10AB at least 6 months before provisional expires
  * **Validity:** 5 years (regular) or 10 years if annual income < ₹5 crore (Finance Act, 2025)
  * **Without 12A:** Foundation taxed at standard corporate rates on surplus income, defeating its purpose



🎁

### 80G Registration (Donor Tax Deduction)

Allows donors to claim tax deduction on donations to PhDSetu Foundation -- critical for fundraising.

  * **Prerequisite:** 12A registration must be obtained first
  * **Application:** Can be filed simultaneously with 12A via Form 10A
  * **Validity:** 5 years (provisional for 3 years, then regular for 5 years)
  * **Deduction:** 50% of donation amount deductible from donor's taxable income (subject to 10% of gross total income limit)
  * **Annual Filing:** File ITR-7 + Form 10BB by October 31 each year



### FCRA Registration (Foreign Contributions)

### Timeline & Requirements

  * **Eligibility:** Foundation must be at least 3 years old and have spent ₹15 lakh on core charitable activities
  * **Application:** Form FC-3 on fcraonline.nic.in. Government fee: ₹5,000
  * **Processing Time:** 90-120 working days from MHA
  * **Mandatory SBI Account:** Designated FCRA account at SBI, New Delhi Main Branch only
  * **Validity:** 5 years, renewable via Form FC-3C (filed 6 months before expiry)
  * **Annual Compliance:** Form FC-4 by December 31 every year (even NIL returns mandatory)
  * **Admin Expense Cap:** Maximum 20% of foreign contributions on administrative expenses
  * **Sub-granting Prohibited:** 2020 amendment prohibits sub-granting foreign funds to other NGOs
  * **Early Alternative:** Apply for Prior Permission (PP) route before 3-year mark if a confirmed foreign donor commitment exists



### FCRA Amendment Bill 2026 -- Watch Closely

A new FCRA Amendment Bill is currently pending in Parliament (as of mid-2026). It proposes automatic cessation of registration upon expiry and a designated authority to take control of assets if registration lapses. Monitor this closely -- it could significantly tighten compliance requirements. Consider engaging a specialised FCRA consultant once the Foundation reaches Year 2.

### GST, TDS & Startup India

💰

### GST Registration

  * Pvt Ltd must register for GST if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for NE/special category states)
  * Register proactively via GST portal -- required for B2B invoicing with recruiters and institutions
  * SaaS/platform fees: taxable at 18% GST (SAC 998314 -- online content)
  * Foundation: Exempt from GST on grant income and donations. GST applies only on any service fees charged.



💳

### TDS Obligations

  * **Mentor Payments:** TDS under Section 194J at 10% (professional services). Deduct before payment, deposit by 7th of next month, file quarterly TDS return (Form 26Q).
  * **Salary:** TDS under Section 192 based on slab rates
  * **Rent:** TDS under Section 194-I (10% for premises)
  * **Contractors/Freelancers:** TDS under Section 194C (1%/2%)
  * Issue Form 16/16A to deductees annually



🏆

### Startup India (DPIIT)

  * **Eligibility:** Pvt Ltd only (Section 8 not eligible). Must be <10 years old, turnover <₹100 crore in any year.
  * **Registration:** Free via nsws.gov.in. Takes 2-10 working days.
  * **Benefits:**
    * 80% rebate on patent/trademark filing fees
    * Self-certification under 9 labour and 3 environmental laws
    * Exemption from Angel Tax (Section 56(2)(viib))
    * EMD waiver for government tenders
  * **80-IAC Tax Holiday:** Separate application to Inter-Ministerial Board after DPIIT recognition. 100% tax exemption on profits for 3 consecutive years out of first 10 years. Requires audited financials and proof of innovation.

## Insurance & Liability

PhDSetu's dual-entity structure and platform model create distinct liability exposures. Insurance mitigates risk and provides credibility to institutional partners and investors.

🛡

### Directors & Officers (D&O) Insurance

  * **Coverage:** Protects directors and officers against personal liability for wrongful acts in their management capacity
  * **Why needed:** Both entities have boards making governance decisions. D&O insurance is increasingly expected by reputed directors and investors.
  * **Recommended coverage:** ₹1-5 crore (increase with growth)
  * **Annual premium estimate:** ₹30,000-1,50,000/year depending on coverage
  * **When to get:** Before first board meeting with external directors or investor term sheet



🔐

### Cyber Liability Insurance

  * **Coverage:** Data breaches, cyber-attacks, ransomware, business interruption from cyber incidents, regulatory penalties, notification costs
  * **Why critical:** PhDSetu handles personal data of scholars (contact info, research profiles), employer data, and payment data. DPDP Act penalties up to ₹250 crore create existential risk.
  * **Recommended coverage:** ₹1-3 crore initially, scaling with user base
  * **Annual premium estimate:** ₹50,000-2,00,000/year
  * **When to get:** Before platform launch to production



📝

### Professional Indemnity Insurance

  * **Coverage:** Claims arising from professional advice or services -- covers negligence, errors, omissions in career guidance or mentorship facilitation
  * **Why relevant:** PhDSetu connects scholars with mentors and provides AI-generated career recommendations. If a scholar claims they were misled, PI insurance covers legal defence costs and settlements.
  * **Recommended coverage:** ₹50 lakh-2 crore
  * **When to get:** Before launching mentorship matching or AI Career Translator



⚠

### Platform Liability Considerations

  * **Mentor Advice:** PhDSetu is a platform, not the advisor. ToS must clearly disclaim liability for mentor advice quality. Mentors are independent contractors, not employees.
  * **Job Listing Accuracy:** PhDSetu does not guarantee job listing accuracy. Employers are responsible for their listings. ToS includes employer representation of accuracy.
  * **AI Career Translator:** AI outputs are advisory, not authoritative. Include clear disclaimers: "AI-generated suggestions are for informational purposes only. Consult with mentors and career advisors before making career decisions."
  * **Intermediary Safe Harbour:** Comply with IT Act, 2000 intermediary guidelines to claim safe harbour from user-generated content liability. Maintain take-down procedures for reported content.
