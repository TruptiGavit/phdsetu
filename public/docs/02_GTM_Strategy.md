## Launch Market Selection

PhD scholar density is not uniform across India. We select five beachhead markets by scoring three variables: **PhD scholar concentration** , **university cluster depth** , and **proximate industry R &D; presence**.

### Selection Methodology

We ranked 15 Indian metro regions on three weighted criteria: PhD enrolments per city (40% weight, sourced from AISHE & NIRF data), number of Tier-1 research institutions within 50 km (30% weight), and number of corporate R&D; centres within 100 km (30% weight). The top five emerged with clear separation from the rest.

Bengaluru is selected as the absolute first market because of an unmatched trifecta: IISc alone produces 400+ PhDs annually, JNCASR and NCBS add another 150+, and the city hosts 300+ corporate R&D; centres. The Bengaluru biocluster is Asia's densest life-sciences research hub. Dr. Sonali's own network in the chemical sciences industry has the deepest connections in this corridor — from Momentive's India operations to Biocon's research leadership. ### Why Not Start Pan-India?

PhDSetu's value proposition depends on density, not breadth. A scholar in Bengaluru needs to see that five other people from their department are already on the platform. They need to see local events, local mentors, and local job postings. Going pan-India from Day 1 would spread every metric thin — engagement, content relevance, mentor availability, employer interest. Handshake grew from 5 universities to 60 to 1,600 — that sequential expansion is the model to follow.

## The First 1,000 Users Acquisition Playbook

The first 1,000 users are not acquired through ads or press. They are earned — one DM, one WhatsApp group, one LinkedIn post at a time. This is the most important phase: every user must feel personally invited.

"Community before platform, conversations before features, trust before transactions."

### The Acquisition Funnel

Each layer requires specific tactics. The funnel is designed to convert Dr. Sonali's growing LinkedIn audience into active community members and eventually platform users.

LinkedIn Followers — Target: 15,000 in 90 daysOrganic content + engagement loops

Engaged Audience (comments/DMs) — Target: 3,00020% engagement rate from followers

WhatsApp/Telegram Community — Target: 1,500CTA in posts → community invite links

Waitlist / Early Adopters — Target: 1,000Personal invitations from community

Day-1 Active Users — Target: 500Complete profile + first interaction

📱

### Step 1: LinkedIn Content Engine (Week 1-4)

  * **Post 4-5x/week** — Tuesday through Saturday, 8:30 AM IST (peak engagement for Indian professionals)
  * **Topic Authority:** Establish 3 pillars — PhD career transitions, research-to-industry stories, and the loneliness of the PhD journey
  * **Format mix:** 60% text posts (personal stories with a hook), 25% carousel/document posts (frameworks, data), 15% video (60-sec raw authenticity clips)
  * **Engagement protocol:** Reply to every comment within 60 minutes. Spend 30 min/day commenting on other PhD-related content. DM every commenter who shares a personal PhD struggle
  * **Specific post topics for Week 1-4:**   
"The day I realised my PhD didn't prepare me for a single job interview"   
"Why every PhD guide should teach CV writing — and none of them do"   
"BARC to Birla to Nivea to Momentive: my 4 career identities"   
"The ₹37,000/month reality — what it means to be a PhD scholar in 2026"   
"If I could tell my PhD self one thing about networking..."



💬

### Step 2: WhatsApp Community Seeding (Week 3-8)

  * **Create 5 discipline-specific WhatsApp groups** (Chemistry & Materials, Life Sciences, Engineering & Tech, Social Sciences, Pure Sciences) — max 256 per group initially
  * **Seed content daily:** One resource share (relevant job/grant/event), one question prompt ("What is the hardest part of your PhD journey this week?"), one mentor spotlight
  * **Rules from Day 1:** No spam, no self-promotion, only PhD-related discussions. Admin-curated. This keeps quality high and makes members feel they are in an exclusive space
  * **Graduate to Telegram** when groups hit 200+ members — Telegram allows larger groups (200K), polls, file sharing, and threaded discussions
  * **Weekly "Ask Me Anything" sessions** on WhatsApp with industry PhDs from Dr. Sonali's network — each session generates 15-20 new joins through word of mouth
  * **Conversion CTA:** Every Friday, share a preview of PhDSetu — "We're building something for all of you. Here's a sneak peek. Want early access?"



🤝

### Step 3: DM-to-Community Pipeline (Week 2-12)

  * **Every LinkedIn DM that mentions PhD struggles** → personal reply + WhatsApp group invite. Target: 20 DMs/day in the first 30 days
  * **Comment mining:** When a post goes viral (1,000+ impressions), systematically review every commenter's profile. PhD scholar? Send a personalised connection request with context
  * **The "PhD Story" content series:** Feature a different scholar's story every week. The featured person shares the post with their network. Each feature adds 50-80 new followers and 10-15 community members
  * **Lab-level penetration:** When one scholar from IISc joins, ask them to bring 3 labmates. Incentivise with "founding member" badge — this creates micro-clusters of trust within institutions



🚀

### Step 4: Waitlist → Launch (Week 8-12)

  * **Launch a simple waitlist page** (single-page PWA with email capture + discipline selection). No product, just promise and positioning
  * **Share the waitlist only in the community first** — "You've been part of this from the beginning. You get first access." This creates exclusivity and urgency
  * **Waitlist gamification:** "Invite 3 PhD friends → move up the queue." Each referral gets tracked, top referrers get direct access to Dr. Sonali for a career consultation
  * **Target: 1,000 waitlist sign-ups before any product is built**. This validates demand, generates an email list, and gives you a launch-day audience
  * **When product launches:** Invite in batches of 100 per week (scarcity drives engagement). Each batch gets a personal welcome email from Dr. Sonali



### The Math: From 0 to 1,000

LinkedIn posts averaging 2,000 impressions × 5 posts/week × 12 weeks = 120,000 total impressions. At a 2% profile-visit rate = 2,400 profile visits. At 25% follow rate = 600 new followers from content alone. Add DM outreach (20/day × 60 days × 15% conversion) = 180. Add WhatsApp group word-of-mouth (5 groups × 40 organic joins each) = 200. Add referral from the waitlist (200 referrers × 3 invites × 20% conversion) = 120. **Total realistic pipeline: 1,100 users in 12 weeks.**

## University Partnership Playbook

Handshake grew to 20 million users by selling career services software to 1,600 universities — making universities the acquisition engine. PhDSetu adapts this model for India's PhD ecosystem: instead of career centres, we target PhD guides and department heads.

Lesson from Handshake

#### The "Ford Focus" Strategy — Adapted for India

Handshake's founders literally drove a Ford Focus from campus to campus, meeting career services directors in person. They sent personalised care packages — mugs with the director's alma mater logo — to secure meetings. The pitch was simple: "Join a unified network that gives your students access to thousands of employers." For PhDSetu, the adaptation is this: Dr. Sonali visits PhD departments in the top 20 universities, meets with the Head of Department or Dean of Research, and positions PhDSetu as free infrastructure that makes their scholars more employable — which directly improves the department's placement statistics and NIRF ranking.

### The Guide Partnership Programme

PhD guides are the gatekeepers. One guide at IISc supervises 4-8 scholars at any time. Win the guide, and you win the entire lab. The Guide Partnership Programme is designed to make guides feel like valued collaborators, not targets of a sales pitch.

📧

### Approach Strategy

  * **Warm introduction:** Use Dr. Sonali's IIT Bombay alumni network to get introductions to department heads. Every IIT has an active alumni WhatsApp group — use those channels
  * **Cold outreach sequence:** Email → LinkedIn message → follow-up email (3-touch sequence over 14 days). Subject line: "Helping your PhD scholars land their ideal career"
  * **Conference corridor:** Attend major Indian academic conferences (ICS, MRSI, CSIR Foundation Day events) and meet guides in person. A 5-minute corridor conversation beats 50 emails
  * **Student-first backdoor:** If a guide is unreachable, onboard their students first. When the guide sees their scholars' profiles on PhDSetu, curiosity follows



🎁

### Value Proposition for Guides

  * **"Your scholars get placed faster"** — direct access to 100+ R&D; employers, curated job matches, and career coaching resources
  * **"Your lab gets visibility"** — a dedicated "Guide Profile" page that showcases their research output, students placed, and lab specialisations. This becomes a recruitment asset
  * **"You get featured"** — inclusion in the "Top PhD Guides in India" directory (highly shareable, SEO-optimised content that guides will proudly link to)
  * **"Zero effort required"** — guides just share a signup link with their students. PhDSetu does everything else
  * **NIRF impact:** Better placement outcomes → higher NIRF ranking for the department. This is the killer argument for department heads



🏆

### Incentive Structure

  * **Bronze Partner (5+ scholars onboarded):** Listed in guide directory, quarterly placement report for their lab
  * **Silver Partner (15+ scholars):** Featured profile, priority access to employer database, invitation to annual PhDSetu Research Conclave
  * **Gold Partner (30+ scholars):** Dedicated account manager, sponsored talk at PhDSetu events, co-branded career workshop for their department
  * **All partners get:** Dashboard showing their scholars' engagement, career interests, and placement status (anonymised aggregate data)

## Referral & Viral Loop Design

PhDSetu's viral loops are engineered into the natural behaviour of its three user types: scholars invite labmates, guides invite students, and mentors invite fellow mentors. Virality is a product feature, not a marketing campaign.

### Three Core Viral Loops

🔬

### Loop 1: The Lab Loop

**Trigger:** A scholar completes their Research Passport profile and receives their first job match.

**Prompt:** "3 of your labmates at [Institution] haven't created their Research Passport yet. Invite them and unlock premium career insights for your lab."

**Mechanics:**

  * One-click WhatsApp share with pre-filled message: "[Name] invited you to PhDSetu — India's career platform for PhD scholars. Create your Research Passport: [link]"
  * **Inviter reward:** Each accepted invite unlocks 1 premium career resource (salary benchmarks, interview guides, etc.)
  * **Invitee reward:** Invited members skip the waitlist and get a personalised profile review within 48 hours
  * **Target K-factor: 0.6-0.8** (each scholar brings ~1 new scholar through direct invites; combined with other channels, K>1)



🎓

### Loop 2: The Guide Loop

**Trigger:** A guide creates their Guide Profile and sees their first scholar's placement activity.

**Prompt:** "Your scholars are getting noticed by employers. Share PhDSetu with your department colleagues — the more guides onboard, the more employers pay attention to your institution."

**Mechanics:**

  * Guide-specific invite link that auto-assigns new scholars to the guide's lab group
  * **Guide reward:** Placement dashboard — guides see anonymised aggregate data on their scholars' career interests, skill gaps, and interview rates
  * **Network effect:** A guide who onboards 10+ scholars sees their department's "Research Visibility Score" increase — this becomes a point of pride
  * **Target: Each guide brings 5-8 scholars + 1-2 peer guides**



🤝

### Loop 3: The Mentor Loop

**Trigger:** A mentor (industry PhD) completes their first mentoring session on PhDSetu.

**Prompt:** "Know other industry PhDs who'd want to give back? Invite them to join PhDSetu's mentor network — building India's largest PhD mentor community together."

**Mechanics:**

  * Mentor invite with co-branding: "You were invited by [Mentor Name] to join PhDSetu's mentor community"
  * **Mentor reward:** "Industry PhDs of India" badge on their LinkedIn (a shareable digital badge that links back to PhDSetu)
  * **Social proof:** "Join 50+ industry PhDs from Tata, Reliance, Biocon, and Google who mentor on PhDSetu" — numbers updated in real-time on the invite page
  * **Target: Each mentor invites 2-3 peer mentors from their company or industry network**
