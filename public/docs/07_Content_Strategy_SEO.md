## Career Playbook Templates

### Playbook Template Structure

Each playbook: 3,000-5,000 words. SEO-optimized title. Structured for both human reading and AI extraction. Target production time: 5-7 days per playbook.

#### Section 1: The Reality Check (500 words)

Honest assessment of the job market for this discipline. Current hiring data, industry demand, salary ranges (entry/mid/senior). Sets expectations without discouraging.

#### Section 2: Career Map -- 6 Paths You Didn't Know About (800 words)

Visual career map showing 6-8 career paths: academia, industry R&D;, government, consulting, entrepreneurship, adjacent fields. For each: description, typical employer, salary range, PhD advantage level.

#### Section 3: Skill Translation Matrix (600 words)

Two-column table: "What you did in your PhD" → "What industry calls it." Specific to the discipline. Reframes thesis work as industry capabilities.

#### Section 4: The 90-Day Action Plan (800 words)

Week-by-week roadmap: Days 1-30 (Profile Building), Days 31-60 (Network Activation), Days 61-90 (Application & Interview). Specific, actionable steps.

#### Section 5: Real Story -- Someone Who Did It (400 words)

Interview excerpt from the "100 Industry PhDs" gallery. Same discipline, similar transition. Makes the abstract concrete.

#### Section 6: Resources & Next Steps (400 words)

CV template download (gated for email), relevant PhDSetu courses, mentor directory link, job board filter link, community group join. Every playbook is a funnel.

## PhD Career Report Methodology

India's first annual PhD Career Outcomes Report -- the single most citable, shareable, and authority-building piece of content PhDSetu will produce. Published free, cited by media, linked by institutions. 

### Survey Design (Radha's Strategy/Sociology Lens)

  * **Target population:** PhD graduates (completed within last 5 years) + current final-year scholars
  * **Sample size:** Minimum 3,000 usable responses (aim 5,000)
  * **Sampling strategy:** Multi-stage stratified -- first by discipline cluster (5 groups), then by institution tier (Central/IIT/State/Private), then by gender and geography
  * **Minimum per subgroup:** 100-150 responses for statistical significance
  * **Survey instrument:** 40-50 questions, 15-20 min completion time
  * **Sections:** Demographics, PhD Experience, Job Search Process, Current Employment, Salary & Compensation, Skills Assessment, Career Satisfaction, Future Plans
  * **Validation:** Pilot test with 50 scholars, expert panel review (3-5 academics)
  * **Data quality:** Attention checks, completion time filtering, duplicate IP detection



### Data Collection & Analysis

  * **Collection channels:** PhDSetu platform, university partnerships, alumni networks, LinkedIn outreach, WhatsApp groups, guide/supervisor network
  * **Incentives:** Free career assessment report, early access to findings, raffle for mentorship sessions
  * **Analysis framework:**
  * Descriptive statistics: salary distributions, sector breakdowns, time-to-employment
  * Cross-tabulations: discipline × career path, institution tier × salary, gender × sector
  * Regression analysis: predictors of career satisfaction, salary drivers
  * Year-over-year trends (from Year 2 onwards)
  * **Sociology lens (Radha):** First-generation scholar outcomes, caste/class intersections, regional university disadvantage analysis, gendered career patterns
  * **Visualizations:** Sankey diagrams (discipline → career path flows), geographic heat maps, salary distribution curves



### 6-Month Production Cycle

#### Month 1: Survey Design & Partnerships

January

Finalize questionnaire (Radha + Dr. Sonali). Secure 20+ university distribution partners. Set up survey platform (Typeform/SurveyMonkey). Pilot test with 50 scholars. Design incentive structure.

#### Month 2-3: Data Collection

February - March

Launch survey across all channels. Weekly progress monitoring (target: 500 responses/week). Mid-cycle boost via social media campaigns and reminder emails. Community mobilization through WhatsApp groups. Guide/supervisor distribution through institutional partnerships.

#### Month 4: Data Cleaning & Analysis

April

Close survey. Clean data (remove incomplete, duplicates, bots). Run full statistical analysis. Generate visualizations. Draft key findings. Radha reviews sociological dimensions.

#### Month 5: Report Writing & Design

May

Write 30-40 page report. Professional design (infographics, charts, pull quotes). Executive summary (2 pages). Key findings one-pager. Media kit preparation. Dr. Sonali writes foreword.

#### Month 6: Launch & Distribution

June

Embargo copies to 10 education journalists. Launch event (webinar). Press release. Social media campaign (30-day drip). Email to all respondents with findings. Pitch to DST/UGC/ANRF for institutional endorsement. Submit data to AISHE.
