## Master Timeline

The canonical phased roadmap from Month 1 to Year 5. All documents should align to these milestones.

Phase 0 — Months 1–3 | Community Only

#### Foundation & Community Building

  * Launch WhatsApp/Telegram PhD communities
  * Co-founders' personal brand activation (Sonali on LinkedIn, all three on Twitter)
  * First 500 community members organically
  * Partnership outreach to 3-5 Bengaluru institutions
  * Legal entity incorporation begins
  * Cost: ₹0 (sweat equity only)



Phase 1 (MVP) — Months 4–8 | PWA Launch

#### Product Build & Early Users

  * PWA development (Next.js + NestJS)
  * Research Passport feature live
  * Community forums launch on platform
  * First mentorship connections via Calendly
  * PhD Career Report survey design starts (Month 6)
  * Target: 2,000+ registered users by Month 8
  * Budget: ₹5–10 Lakhs



Phase 2 (Growth) — Months 9–18 | Revenue Starts

#### Monetization & National Expansion

  * Employer portal launches — first recruiter subscriptions
  * Expand to Delhi-NCR, Mumbai-Pune, Chennai-Hyderabad
  * PhD Career Report launches on National Science Day (Feb 28, Year 2)
  * Premium courses go live (₹999–4,999)
  * Institutional partnerships with 10+ universities
  * Native app trigger: 5,000+ active users
  * Break-even target: Month 16–18
  * Budget: ₹20–40 Lakhs



Phase 3 (Scale) — Months 19–30 | National Platform + App

#### Scale & Dominance

  * Native mobile app launch (iOS + Android)
  * AI-powered job matching engine
  * Vernacular UI rollout: Hindi first, then regional languages
  * Kolkata-Kharagpur and other Phase 2 cities
  * Corporate R&D; partnerships at scale
  * 25,000+ active users by end of Year 2



Years 3–5 | Market Leadership

#### Dominance, Global & Diversification

  * 75,000 active users (Year 3) → 2,00,000 (Year 5)
  * Revenue: ₹3–5 Crore (Y3) → ₹15–25 Crore (Y5)
  * Global expansion begins (SE Asia, Middle East first)
  * Full vernacular support (6+ languages)
  * PhDSetu becomes India's definitive PhD career platform
  * Deepstambh social impact at significant scale

#### Revenue projections vary across scenarios

The Financial Model contains base/optimistic/conservative scenarios. The Investor Pitch Deck uses optimistic projections for maximum impact. Readers should be aware these are different scenarios.

✓ Canonical (Base Case): Y1 ₹15–30L, Y2 ₹80–150L, Y3 ₹3–5Cr, Y5 ₹15–25Cr

Found in: Multiple documents

#### PhD graduates per year — "40,000" vs "30,000-50,000"

Some documents cite "40,000 PhDs annually" as a round number, while the range is 30,000-50,000. Both are acceptable but the range is more accurate.

✓ Canonical: 30,000–50,000 PhDs graduating annually (use range for precision, 40,000+ for shorthand)

Found in: Multiple documents

#### User growth targets — active vs registered

Some docs cite "10,000 users in Year 1" (registered) while others say "5,000" (active). These are different measures.

✓ Canonical: Y1 = 5,000 active users / ~10,000 registered. "Active" = logged in within last 30 days.

Found in: 06_Technical_Architecture.html, PhDSetu_Master_Brainstorming.html

#### Tech stack specifics — minor variations in tool choices

✓ Canonical: Custom PWA (Next.js + NestJS). Use Calendly for mentorship booking, Zoom for video. Custom community forums and profiles.

Found in: 04_Financial_Model.html, PhDSetu_Master_Brainstorming.html

#### Phase budget figures — minor rounding differences

Phase 1 budget cited as "₹5-10L" in some places and "₹8-12L" in the detailed financial model. The difference reflects inclusive vs exclusive of co-founder stipends.

✓ Canonical: Phase 1 (MVP) = ₹5-10L (development costs, excluding co-founder stipends)
