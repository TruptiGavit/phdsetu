## Government Engagement Strategy

How to position PhDSetu as an implementation partner to India's apex research bodies -- complementing, never competing with, their mandates. 

#### 🏳 ANRF -- Anusandhan National Research Foundation

ANRF, established through the ANRF Act 2023, is the apex body providing strategic direction for research and innovation across India. Its mandate includes forging collaborations among industry, academia, and government. PhDSetu's positioning: **implementation partner for career development outcomes** -- ANRF funds research; PhDSetu ensures the researchers find careers worthy of their training. 

  * **PAIR Programme Integration:** ANRF's hub-and-spoke mentorship model (top-tier institutions mentoring emerging universities) needs career services at spoke institutions. PhDSetu becomes the career services layer for all PAIR spoke universities, providing Research Passport profiles, CV workshops, and industry connection pathways.
  * **Career Outcomes Data Partner:** ANRF is mandated to undertake an annual survey of outcomes of scientific research. PhDSetu's PhD Career Report provides exactly this data on career outcomes, employment timelines, salary benchmarks, and sector distribution -- offered as a co-branded deliverable.
  * **RDI Fund Talent Connector:** ANRF's ₹1 lakh crore Research, Development & Innovation Fund needs PhDs flowing into funded companies. PhDSetu becomes the talent pipeline for RDI-funded entities.
  * **Catalytic Partnership Program:** For ANRF's large-scale public-private partnerships (minimum ₹100 Cr projects), PhDSetu positions as the human capital mobilization partner -- sourcing PhDs for project teams.



#### 🏫 UGC -- University Grants Commission

The UGC regulates all central and state universities. Its NET/JRF examinations and fellowship programs directly touch every PhD scholar in the country. PhDSetu's positioning: **career readiness platform complementing UGC fellowship programs**. 

  * **Post-Fellowship Career Transition:** UGC-NET/JRF fellows have 5-year funding windows. PhDSetu provides transition planning from Year 3, ensuring fellows don't face career voids after fellowship completion.
  * **Institutional Quality Signal:** UGC can recommend PhDSetu access as part of institutional career services, much like it mandates anti-plagiarism software (Urkund/Turnitin) -- making career development infrastructure a quality metric.
  * **NEP 2020 Implementation:** NEP's focus on research-intensive universities creates demand for career development infrastructure. PhDSetu positions as the NEP-aligned career services standard.
  * **PhD Regulation Compliance:** UGC's PhD regulations require skill development components. PhDSetu's workshops and industry connect programs fulfil this mandate.



#### 🔬 DST -- Department of Science & Technology

DST administers SERB grants, INSPIRE fellowships, and now oversees ESTIC (the new Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave replacing the Indian Science Congress). PhDSetu's positioning: **human capital analytics partner**. 

  * **INSPIRE Fellow Tracking:** DST's INSPIRE program (Innovation in Science Pursuit for Inspired Research) creates thousands of research fellows. PhDSetu tracks career outcomes of INSPIRE alumni, providing DST with programme effectiveness data.
  * **ESTIC Conclave Integration:** The new ESTIC (first held November 2025 at Bharat Mandapam) replaces the Indian Science Congress. PhDSetu positions for a career development track at ESTIC -- a PhD career fair within the national science conclave.
  * **Women in STEM Data:** DST's initiatives for women in STEM (WOS-A, WOS-B, WOS-C, KIRAN) need outcome tracking. PhDSetu provides gender-disaggregated career data and targeted career programming.
  * **S &T; Indicator Reports:** DST publishes national R&D; statistics. PhDSetu's career data enriches India's S&T; indicators with human capital metrics.



#### 🔬 CSIR & DBT

CSIR's 37 national laboratories and DBT's biotech research mandates produce thousands of PhD scholars. PhDSetu's positioning: **lab-to-career bridge**. 

  * **CSIR Lab Placement Cell:** CSIR labs (NCL Pune, CCMB Hyderabad, IICT Hyderabad, NPL Delhi, CDRI Lucknow) produce 2,000+ PhDs annually. PhDSetu becomes the shared placement platform across all 37 labs.
  * **DBT-BIRAC Startup Pipeline:** PhDs with entrepreneurial ambitions are connected to DBT's BIRAC (Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council) for startup funding and incubation.
  * **CSIR-Industry Interface:** CSIR's mandate to translate research to industry is directly served by PhDSetu's corporate R&D; partnerships -- scholars become the human connection between CSIR research and industry application.



### The Golden Rule: Complement, Never Compete

Every government engagement must follow one principle: PhDSetu is infrastructure, not a funding body. We don't compete with ANRF for grants, with UGC for regulations, or with DST for programme design. We are the career development layer that makes their investments in human capital produce measurable career outcomes. Our pitch to every government body is the same: "You fund the research. You set the standards. We ensure the researchers find careers worthy of that investment."

## International Expansion Partnerships

Learning from EURAXESS: a model study of the world's largest researcher mobility platform, and how PhDSetu can build an Indian equivalent with global interoperability. 

### EURAXESS Model Study

EURAXESS spans 43 European countries with 9 worldwide hubs and 600+ Service Centres. It provides free, personalised information on career development, job listings, funding navigation, and relocation support. Key features PhDSetu can adapt:

  * • **Service Centre Network:** EURAXESS has 600+ physical service centres at universities. PhDSetu can build a distributed presence through university career cells, starting with 50 Premium/Standard tier universities.
  * • **Career Development Plan Tool:** EURAXESS provides structured CDP tools for researchers. PhDSetu builds the "Research Passport" as an equivalent -- but adapted for Indian academic and industry contexts.
  * • **Beyond Academia Module:** EURAXESS's programme for researchers exploring non-academic careers mirrors PhDSetu's core value proposition. PhDSetu goes deeper by providing domain-specific career mapping.
  * • **International Mentoring Programme:** EURAXESS matches researchers with mentors across borders. PhDSetu builds an Indian diaspora mentor network (Indian-origin scientists at global universities and companies).



### PhDSetu International Strategy

Rather than competing with EURAXESS, PhDSetu becomes its natural Indian counterpart -- an interoperable platform that helps Indian scholars access European (and global) opportunities while helping the diaspora give back.

  * • **EURAXESS Interoperability:** API-level integration so Indian scholars can browse EURAXESS opportunities through PhDSetu, and European institutions can source Indian PhD talent.
  * • **DAAD Partnership:** Germany is the top European destination for Indian researchers. DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service) can co-fund career guidance content for Germany-bound scholars.
  * • **British Council -- Study UK GREAT:** UK's researcher attraction programmes, including the new Global Talent Visa research route, can be directly featured on PhDSetu.
  * • **Fulbright India:** Co-branded content on US academic opportunities. Fulbright alumni become PhDSetu mentors.
  * • **UNESCO Science Report Integration:** PhDSetu contributes Indian PhD career data to UNESCO's periodic Science Reports, gaining international visibility.
  * • **JSPS / Humboldt Foundation:** Japan and Germany fellowship programmes integrated into PhDSetu's international mobility section.



### The "PhDSetu Abroad" Module

A dedicated section of the platform that aggregates international postdoc listings, faculty positions, and R&D; roles specifically relevant to Indian PhD scholars. Unlike generic portals, it includes visa guidance (which visa category, processing times, common pitfalls), cost-of-living calculators, and stories from Indian PhDs who successfully moved abroad. Immigration consultant partners (Section 06) are integrated here for seamless one-click consultation booking. This module alone can drive ₹1Cr+ annual revenue through referral commissions and premium content.

Standardised Memorandum of Understanding frameworks for each partnership type, aligned with UGC academic collaboration regulations and Indian legal standards. These templates follow the structure used by University of Delhi's generic MoU format and DAAD Indo-German partnership frameworks. 

### Template A: University Partnership MoU

#### 📄 Preamble & Parties

This Memorandum of Understanding establishes a collaborative relationship between [University Name], represented by its Registrar/Vice Chancellor, and PhDSetu (operated by [Legal Entity Name]), represented by its Co-Founder & CEO, Dr. Sonali. The general purpose is to establish mutual cooperation for the career development of doctoral scholars enrolled at the University.

#### 🎯 Article I: Purpose of Agreement

  * To provide structured career development services to PhD scholars of the University
  * To facilitate industry connections and placement support for graduating doctoral candidates
  * To conduct career workshops, mentoring programmes, and industry readiness assessments
  * To generate and share career outcome data for institutional quality improvement



#### 🛠 Article II: Scope of Cooperation

  * PhDSetu shall provide platform access to all registered PhD scholars of the University at the agreed partnership tier (Foundation / Standard / Premium)
  * PhDSetu shall conduct [number] on-campus career workshops per academic year
  * The University shall designate a faculty coordinator as the institutional point of contact
  * Both parties shall cooperate in data collection for the annual PhD Career Report
  * Career fairs and research showcases shall be organized at mutually agreed schedules



#### 💰 Article III: Financial Arrangements

  * The University shall pay PhDSetu an annual partnership fee as per the agreed tier: Foundation (Free) / Standard (₹1-3L) / Premium (₹3-5L)
  * Placement success fees, if applicable, shall be 5-8% of first-year CTC for placements facilitated exclusively through PhDSetu
  * Workshop costs beyond the tier allocation shall be borne as per a mutually agreed sub-agreement
  * Revenue from co-branded content or reports shall be shared as per separate commercial terms



#### 🔒 Article IV: Data Privacy & IP

  * Scholar data shared with PhDSetu shall be used exclusively for career development services
  * PhDSetu shall comply with applicable data protection laws and university data governance policies
  * Aggregated, anonymised career outcome data may be published in the PhD Career Report with University's written consent
  * Neither party shall use the other's trademarks or logos without prior written approval



#### ⏳ Article V: Duration & Termination

  * This MoU shall be effective for an initial period of two (2) years from the date of signing
  * It may be renewed for successive one-year terms by mutual written agreement
  * Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice
  * Ongoing scholar services shall continue through the academic year of termination

### Partnership Health Scorecard

Each partnership is evaluated quarterly using a five-dimension health scorecard:

### Engagement Depth

How actively does the partner engage beyond the contractual minimum? Do they attend events, share content, refer other partners? Score 1-10.

### Scholar Impact

How many scholars directly benefited from this partnership? Measured in profile views, workshop attendance, placement outcomes, and content engagement.

### Revenue Contribution

Direct and indirect revenue generated. Direct: partnership fees, referral commissions. Indirect: placements facilitated leading to success fees, brand value increase.

### Strategic Value

Does this partnership open doors to other high-value partnerships? Government endorsement unlocks universities. IIT partnership unlocks corporates. Score 1-10.

### Renewal Likelihood

Based on engagement, satisfaction surveys, and relationship health. Green (>80% likely), Yellow (50-80%), Red (<50%). Triggers proactive outreach for Yellow/Red partners.

### Operational Efficiency

How much internal effort does this partnership require vs. value delivered? Partnerships requiring high effort for low value are candidates for tier downgrade or sunset.
