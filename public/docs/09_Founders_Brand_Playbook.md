### Voice & Tone Guidelines

#### The Voice Is...

**Warm but authoritative.** She speaks like a senior mentor over tea, not a corporate trainer in a conference room. Direct without being preachy. Data-informed without being dry. Personal without being self-indulgent. The voice of someone who has been where you are -- and made it to the other side.

#### The Voice Avoids...

**Corporate jargon, motivational clich es, and LinkedIn-bro energy.** No "Here's what I learnt from failing 27 times." No "10x your career." No humble-bragging. The audience is academic -- they are trained to detect inauthenticity. Respect their intelligence. Say something real.

### The "Portfolio Scientist" Narrative Framework

Every piece of content Dr. Sonali creates should map back to one of three narrative threads:  
  
**1\. The Bridge Builder:** She has walked the bridge between academia and industry -- not once, but four times. Each organisation taught her a different dimension of what research careers can become.  
  
**2\. The System Critic:** She sees how broken the PhD-to-career pipeline is in India, and she has the credibility to name the problems specifically. Not ranting -- diagnosing.  
  
**3\. The Architect:** She is not just pointing out problems. She is building PhDSetu -- the infrastructure that should have existed all along. Every post should hint at a larger project unfolding.

## Personal Brand -> Platform Conversion

The most critical transition: moving from "Dr. Sonali the LinkedIn creator" to "Dr. Sonali, co-founder & CEO of PhDSetu" without losing authenticity or audience trust. This is a gradual narrative shift, not a sudden pivot.

"The audience should never feel they were being marketed to all along. They should feel they were part of a journey -- and the platform is the natural destination of that journey."

### The Three-Phase Transition

Phase 1: Months 1-3 -- Pure Personal Brand

#### Build Trust Before Building Product

100% personal brand content. No product mentions. No "sign up" CTAs. Just share expertise, tell stories, build community. The only mention of PhDSetu is in her bio and occasional "I'm working on something for PhD scholars" hints.  
  
**The goal:** When she eventually announces PhDSetu, the reaction should be "Finally!" not "Oh, she was selling something."

  * Post 3x/week: career advice, personal stories, ecosystem analysis
  * Build WhatsApp community to 300+ scholars
  * Run 2 LinkedIn Lives (pure Q&A;, no product pitch)
  * Plant seeds: "I've been thinking about building something for PhD scholars..."



Phase 2: Months 4-6 -- Build in Public

#### Show the Platform Emerging

80% personal brand, 20% build-in-public content. Share what you're learning from conversations with PhD scholars. Show wireframes. Ask the community for input. Make them feel like co-creators, not customers.  
  
**The goal:** The audience should feel invested in PhDSetu's success because they helped shape it.

  * Monthly "building PhDSetu" update posts
  * Share survey results and community insights
  * Ask for feature suggestions publicly
  * Profile headline evolves: add "Building PhDSetu"



Phase 3: Months 7-12 -- Platform Launch & Integration

#### From Creator to Founder

60% personal brand, 30% PhDSetu content, 10% promotional. The personal brand now explicitly serves the platform. Every career advice post ends with "This is exactly the kind of support PhDSetu is building." The conversion is organic because the audience has watched it unfold.  
  
**The goal:** 1,000+ users on PhDSetu within 30 days of launch, drawn directly from her personal brand audience.

  * Soft launch: invite-only beta for community members
  * Hard launch: LinkedIn carousel + LinkedIn Live + press release
  * Launch week: daily posts for 5 consecutive days
  * Convert personal audience to platform waitlist/users



### Announcement Strategy

### Soft Launch (Month 8)

  * Exclusive beta access for first 500 community members
  * "You've been part of this journey. Now you get first access."
  * Personal DM to most engaged followers
  * LinkedIn post: "After 8 months of building in public, PhDSetu is live for our founding community."
  * No press, no PR. Just community-first launch.

### Hard Launch (Month 10)

  * Press release to targeted media (see Module 07)
  * LinkedIn carousel: "PhDSetu is open. Here's what it does."

  * Target: 1,000 signups in first 7 days



### The Personal Brand Never Stops

After PhDSetu launches, the co-founders' personal brands do not become "company accounts." Sonali continues to post as herself -- with opinions, stories, and insights that a company page could never carry. Radha posts as the academic-practitioner. Trupti posts as the builder. The personal brands feed the platform, and the platform gives each co-founder fresh stories, data, and community interactions to draw from. This flywheel never stops spinning. The moment any of them becomes "just a co-founder posting company updates," the magic dies. They must always remain the scientists/builders who talk to you like mentors, not marketers.
