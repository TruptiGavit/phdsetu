### Salary Strategy: Competitive Cash + Generous ESOPs + Mission Premium

PhDSetu cannot compete with unicorn salaries. Our compensation strategy: pay at or slightly above the competitive early-stage startup bracket (avoiding service-company rates), offer generous ESOP grants to early employees (the "founding team premium"), and lean into the mission premium -- mission-driven candidates will accept 10-15% lower cash for meaningful work. Every offer letter should include a clear ESOP explanation and a personal note from the relevant co-founder on why this role matters.

## Culture Document

Culture is not perks, ping-pong tables, or unlimited snacks. At PhDSetu, culture is the set of non-negotiable beliefs that guide every decision when no one is watching.

🎓

#### Scholar-First

Every product decision, every feature priority, every partnership starts with one question: "Does this make a PhD scholar's life meaningfully better?" If the answer is unclear, we don't ship it.

💡

#### Mission Over Metrics

We track engagement, revenue, and growth -- but we optimise for impact. A scholar who lands their first industry interview through PhDSetu matters more than a thousand passive signups.

🔍

#### Radical Transparency

Salaries are benchmarked openly. ESOP terms are explained in plain language. Company finances are shared in monthly all-hands. No information asymmetry between founder and team.

🌍

#### Inclusive by Design

The scholar in Nagpur is as important as the postdoc at IISc. We build for regional universities, first-generation researchers, women in STEM, and scholars with disabilities. Inclusion is a product feature, not a checkbox.

🔧

#### Build > Talk

Ship small, ship often, get feedback. A working prototype beats a 50-slide deck. We value doers over planners, and we measure output by impact on scholars, not by hours logged.

💜

#### Respect the Journey

PhDs have spent 4-7 years in deep intellectual work. We never patronise, never oversimplify, and never treat scholars as "customers to convert." We serve as allies, not vendors.

### The PhDSetu Hiring Test

Before extending any offer, every candidate must pass the "Scholar Empathy Test": Can they explain, in their own words, what makes the PhD experience in India uniquely challenging? If they can't articulate why a PhD scholar from a regional university needs different support than an IIT graduate, they are not the right fit -- regardless of their technical skills. Skills can be taught. Empathy for the mission cannot.

## Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

PhDSetu is remote-first by design -- because the scholars we serve are distributed across India, and so should our team be. Structured flexibility with clear accountability.

#### 🏠 Work Model

  * **Default: Remote-first.** All roles are remote unless explicitly designated hybrid (Sales/Partnerships, Operations).
  * Hybrid roles: 2-3 days/week in a co-working space in Mumbai or Pune, aligned with client meetings and team collaboration days.
  * Annual team offsite: 3-5 days twice a year for strategic planning, bonding, and culture reinforcement. Budget: ₹15K-25K per person per offsite.
  * Co-working budget: ₹3K-5K/month for any remote employee who prefers working from a co-working space.



#### ⏰ Core Hours & Availability

  * **Core hours: 10:00 AM -- 4:00 PM IST** -- all team members must be available for synchronous communication during these 6 hours.
  * Flexible hours: remaining 2-3 hours can be worked at the employee's convenience (early morning, late evening).
  * Expected response time: 30 minutes during core hours for Slack/Teams messages; 2 hours for email.
  * No-meeting blocks: Tuesday and Thursday afternoons are protected for deep work. No meetings scheduled.



#### 💻 Equipment & Setup

  * Laptop: Company-provided MacBook Air/Pro or equivalent Windows laptop (₹60K-90K budget).
  * Home office stipend: One-time ₹10K for desk, chair, monitor, or accessories.
  * Internet reimbursement: ₹1K/month for broadband (minimum 50 Mbps required).
  * Power backup: Employees in areas with frequent outages should have UPS/inverter (₹5K one-time reimbursement).



#### 🔒 Security & Compliance

  * VPN mandatory for accessing all production systems and internal tools.
  * Multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all accounts: email, Slack, GitHub, cloud dashboards.
  * No work from public Wi-Fi without VPN. Personal hotspot as backup.
  * Scholar data (personal profiles, CVs) is classified as sensitive -- DPDP Act 2023 compliance required.
  * Annual security awareness training for all team members.



#### 🌴 Leave & Wellbeing

  * **Paid leave:** 24 days/year (inclusive of casual + earned leave). Flexible use, no distinction.
  * **Sick leave:** 10 days/year. No documentation required for single-day leaves.
  * **Mental health days:** 2 days/quarter, no questions asked. Just mark the calendar.
  * **Parental leave:** 26 weeks (maternity, per law) + 4 weeks (paternity, paid). Adoption leave: 12 weeks.
  * **Sabbatical:** After 2 years, eligible for 2-week unpaid sabbatical for personal projects, research, or travel.
  * After-hours communication boundary: no Slack messages after 8 PM unless urgent (defined as "production outage or scholar safety issue").



#### 📈 Performance & Reviews

  * Outcome-based evaluation, not hours-logged or presence-based tracking.
  * Monthly 1-on-1s with direct manager (30 min) + quarterly goal reviews.
  * OKRs set quarterly, aligned with company-level objectives. Shared transparently across the team.
  * 360-degree feedback annually -- peers, manager, self-assessment, and (for community-facing roles) scholar feedback.
  * Annual compensation review in April, benchmarked against updated market data.

  * **Minimum 1-year cliff** -- mandated by Rule 12(6)(a), Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014. No acceleration below this.

#### Tax Implications for Employees

  * **Stage 1 -- Exercise:** Perquisite tax on (FMV at exercise − exercise price), taxed as salary income at slab rates.
  * **Stage 2 -- Sale:** Capital gains tax on (sale price − FMV at exercise). LTCG at 12.5% if held >24 months.
  * **DPIIT deferral:** If PhDSetu gets DPIIT recognition + 80-IAC certification, employees can defer perquisite tax up to 48-60 months from allotment.
  * **Action item:** Apply for DPIIT recognition at incorporation. This significantly reduces the tax burden on early employees exercising options.
  * **Education:** Hold an ESOP education session with every new hire. No surprises at tax time.

## Diversity & Inclusion Hiring Goals

PhDSetu builds for all of India's PhD scholars. Our team must reflect that diversity -- not as a checkbox, but as a strategic advantage. People who have lived the challenges we're solving build better solutions.

### Gender Diversity

40%+

Women and non-binary team members across all functions, not concentrated in community/content roles only. Target at least one woman engineer by Phase 2.

### Geographic Diversity

50%+

Team members from Tier 2/3 cities or regional university backgrounds. If the team is entirely from IITs and metros, it cannot empathise with the scholars who need PhDSetu most.

### PhD Holders on Team

30%+

At least 30% of the team should hold a PhD or be current scholars. Community managers, content leads, and moderators are natural PhD-holder roles.

#### Inclusive Hiring Practices

  * Blind resume screening for tech roles -- remove names, photos, institution names in first round
  * Structured interviews with standardised rubrics -- every candidate evaluated on the same criteria
  * At least one diverse panellist in every final-round interview
  * Flexible interview scheduling (accommodate part-time PhD scholars, different time zones)
  * Take-home assignments over whiteboard coding -- more inclusive, less biased toward interview performance
  * Explicitly state in every JD: "We encourage applications from scholars at regional universities, first-generation researchers, and candidates from underrepresented backgrounds"



#### Inclusion Beyond Hiring

  * Vernacular-friendly internal communication -- Hindi/regional language use in casual channels is welcome
  * Flexible religious/cultural leave -- swap public holidays for personally significant dates
  * Accessibility: all internal tools and documents must be screen-reader compatible
  * Pronouns in Slack profiles and email signatures -- normalised, not mandated
  * Quarterly inclusion check-in: anonymous survey on belonging, safety, and voice
  * Zero-tolerance policy on caste discrimination, gender bias, or institutional elitism (IIT vs non-IIT hierarchies)
